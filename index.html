How automatic scheduling works

The plan is deliberately transparent, so its results stay easy to review.

1

Availability comes first

Workers are never placed on a day, day/morning shift, or night shift they marked unavailable.

2

24h shifts are optional and safe

A 24h pair is used only if it improves the hour-target balance. When selected, the generator keeps the configured recovery days clear before another 24h shift.

3

Minimise hour-target error

The generator minimises the sum of squared differences between requested and scheduled hours. Shift preferences only break an equal-score tie.
